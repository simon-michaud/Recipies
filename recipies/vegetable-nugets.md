# Vegetable Nuggets

**Preparation:** 15 minutes

**Cooking time:** 15 minutes

## Ingredients

* 1 medium grated courgette
* 1 medium potato
* or
* 1 medium sweet potato
* 1 medium grated carrot
* 1 medium grated parsnip

* ~1/3 cup old cheddar cheese
* 2 shallots finely diced
* 1 egg
* 2 teaspoon oil
* salt & pepper

## Directions

* Mix all ingredients together (add 2-3 tbsp breadcrumbs or flour if the mixture is too wet to hold shape), bake in oven at 400°F for ~20 minutes on a baking sheet, turn once at mid-point.

> Nuggets can be frozen and re-heated in oven at 375°F for ~10 minutes.
