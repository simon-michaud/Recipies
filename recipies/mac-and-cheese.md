# Mac and Cheese

**Preparation:** 10 minutes

**Cooking time:** 30 minutes

## Ingredients

- 2 cups uncooked macaroni noodles
- pinch of salt
- 2.5 to 3 cups milk
- 0.25 teaspoon garlic powder
- 0.5 teaspoon onion powder
- 1 tablespoon butter
- 0.5 teaspoon pepper, to taste
- 0.25 cup water
- 2 tablespoons cream cheese or sour cream or crème fraiche
- 1 to 1.5 cup cheddar cheese shredded

## Steps

1. In a saucepan combine a pinch of salt, milk, garlic powder, onion powder, butter and water. Bring to a boil.
1. Add in macaroni noodles, reduce heat to low.
1. Continue to simmer stirring frequently until noodles are cooked (about 20 minutes).
1. Watch to make sure the liquid doesn’t completely evaporate. You can add extra milk ¼ cup at a time if the liquid is low.
1. Once the pasta is cooked, ensure the noodles are not dry. They should be moist before adding cheese. Stir in cream cheese until melted.
1. Remove from heat and stir in cheddar cheese. Season with pepper to taste. Cover for 3 minutes.
