# Greek Pork Souvlaki

**Preparation:** 15 minutes + overnight marinating

**Cooking time:** 12 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1½ lbs pork tenderloin, cut into 1-inch cubes
- ¼ cup extra virgin olive oil
- 3 tbsp lemon juice
- 2 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp dried oregano
- 1 tbsp fresh thyme
- 1 tsp grated lemon zest
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp ground black pepper
- 6 wooden skewers

## Directions

1. Whisk olive oil, lemon juice, vinegar, garlic, oregano, thyme, lemon zest, salt, and pepper together in a bowl.
1. Add pork cubes and toss to coat. Cover and refrigerate overnight.
1. Soak wooden skewers in water for 30 minutes before grilling.
1. Preheat grill to medium-high heat.
1. Thread pork onto skewers and discard marinade.
1. Grill for 10-12 minutes, turning every 2-3 minutes, until fully cooked through.
1. Serve immediately with tzatziki sauce.

> Makes 6 servings.
