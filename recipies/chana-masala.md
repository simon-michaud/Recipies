# Chana Masala

**Preparation:** 15 minutes

**Cooking time:** 5 hours

## Ingredients

- 15 garlic cloves
- 1 onion
- 1.5L (approx. 2 x 796ml cans) diced tomatoes
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 tbsp curry powder
- 1 tbsp turmeric
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 1 tsp flaked hot peppers
- 3 tbsp beef broth or 2 tbsp dark miso paste
- 3 cups chickpeas
- canola oil

## Steps

1. Make garlic confit by crushing it and cooking it in a cast iron cooker with canola oil at 300F for ~2 hours.  Once done, remove it to reuse cooker.
1. Fry diced onion in cooker until it goldens (add oil if necessary).
1. Start oven at 350F
1. Deglaze cooker with the tomatoes juice, add all ingredients except chickpeas and mix thoroughly.
1. Put in oven for an hour.
1. Add chickpeas and cook for 2 hours more, mixing it every 30 minutes.


> It can be kept in Mason jars for months if refrigerated once sealed.
