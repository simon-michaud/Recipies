~250g prosciutto
~250g bacon
~175g spicy Calabrese sausage

Cut into small pieces:
~6 green onions
~4 garlic cloves

1 pint of 15% cream

Parmesan cheese

Egg yolk for each plate

Each ingredient can be adjusted to taste.

Sauté the green onions and garlic in the bacon, then add the other meats. When they seem cooked through, add the cream and simmer for a bit, allowing the cream to combine with the remaining fat. I add Parmesan cheese to the sauce and add it to the plate with the egg yolk.
