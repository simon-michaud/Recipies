# Mymy Pasta

**Preparation:** 15 minutes

**Cooking time:** 15 minutes

## Ingredients

- Pasta of your choice
- ~250g prosciutto, chopped in 1 cm pieces
- ~250g bacon, chopped in 1 cm pieces
- ~175g spicy Calabrese sausage, chopped in 1 cm pieces
- ~6 shallots, finely diced
- ~4 garlic cloves, pureed
- 1 pint of 15% cream
- Parmesan cheese, to taste
- 1 egg yolk per plate

Each ingredient can be adjusted to taste.

## Steps

1. Sauté the shallots and garlic in the bacon, then add the other meats.
1. When they seem cooked through, add the cream and simmer for a bit, allowing the cream to combine with the remaining fat.
1. Add Parmesan cheese to the sauce.
1. Plate the pasta and top with sauce and an egg yolk in the center of each plate.
